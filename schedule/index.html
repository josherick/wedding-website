Saturday, August 1, 2026
Ceremony & Reception
3:30pm

Timeline

The ceremony will begin at 3:30pm.

After the ceremony, we'll have a cocktail hour with light bites. Then, we'll move to the barn for a seated dinner, followed by dancing until 10pm (with some late night bites)!

What to Wear

We request that you wear cocktail to formal attire (think suits, dresses, or comparable alternatives). Please avoid wearing shades of white, ivory, or cream, as these colors are reserved for the bride.

Our ceremony will be outdoors, weather permitting, and our reception will be in a barn. Evenings in Northern Michigan are occasionally a bit chilly, so you may want to bring an extra layer for later in the evening.

Wedding Shuttle Service

We'll have shuttles running to four hotels on August 1. See Travel & Accommodations for shuttle pickup times and locations.
